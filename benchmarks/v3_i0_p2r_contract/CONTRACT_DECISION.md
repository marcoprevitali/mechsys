## What is justified

- Initial exact structure/model/state and per-contribution comparisons can be
  separated from native unordered reduction. Candidate source identity is
  pinned to its own expected SHA; baseline and candidate SHAs need not match.
- For the witness each first scalar sum obeys E=gamma93*sum_abs with u=2^-53;
  two identical rounded-term lists give pair budget2E. This includes neither
  contribution formation nor evolved-state/physical-error allowances.
- Actual CUDA translation stores x_new=2x-xb+h^2F/m and the velocity centered
  at OLD x time. Its matched-input force-induced vS uncertainty is h*D_F/(2m),
  while the half-step-velocity change has h*D_F/m. No ideal-VV replacement.
- Actual rotation uses wb/wa, two ORDERED quaternion products and normalization;
  the conditional bilinear/norm-floor bounds retain gyroscopic,frame and
  rounding dependencies. Force-only and torque-only first-step scales can be
  derived without using observed mismatches as tolerances.
- Streaming is a permutation in the frozen periodic fixture, not another
  force sum. Collision/moments and the next imprint close a nontrivial feedback
  loop; exact structural/branch conditions must precede continuous bounds.

## Critical unresolved items / stop condition

1. **Physical startup convention:** source xb=x-h*v_seed,wb=w_seed has no
   acceleration-based staggered bootstrap. Operational stages are unambiguous,
   but treating every supplied/reported velocity as an endpoint physical value
   is unjustified. Discrete-map compatibility is not physical accuracy.
2. **Certified local enclosures:** actual m/I,primitive rounding graphs,norm/
   density lower bounds and their one-step boxes are not yet fully pinned and
   certified for this proposed contract. The frozen probe omits I; the source
   construction is known and can be enclosed, but no closed acceptance table
   is inferred from analytic inertia or observed omega alone.
3. **Two-step feedback/branches:** no enclosure proves all occupancy/eligibility/
   image/rebuild/limiter predicates remain on the registered branch throughout
   the propagated boxes. Near tangency and contact thresholds defeat an
   unqualified smooth Lipschitz assertion. A symbolic M_1 or observed matching
   cells is not a proof. Full nonduplicated budget allocation must also fit
   unchanged independent exchange limits.

These items activate the requested stop rather than a larger tolerance. No
complete one-step or two-step trajectory gate can be declared justified yet.
No new history/event tests were executed or accepted. The current observer's
empty histories and two-step x/v agreement do not supply the missing proof.
