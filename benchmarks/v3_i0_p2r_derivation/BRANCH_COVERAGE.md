# Branch coverage and unproved obligations

CSV `status=PASS` requires a strict sign AND certified premises. A strict sign
in a conditional source/contraction-family graph has status UNRESOLVED, with
its strict_sign_status and basis recorded separately. This prevents a positive
conditional margin from becoming a fictitious compiled branch PASS. Internal
branches absent from the historical observer have observed_branch=NOT_CAPTURED.
Source/count premises are labeled as such, not new simulation observations.

Missing proofs are UNRESOLVED, not assigned arbitrary finite intervals. No
claim is made that the CSV is an exhaustive compiled SASS branch certificate.
The exact known input branches can remain deterministic while a required
strict-sign or compiled-correspondence proof is incomplete. Those meanings
must not be conflated with a physical contact event or race diagnosis.
